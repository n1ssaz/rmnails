01 — Philosophy

Details matter. So do your hands.

The Russian technique is slow on purpose: careful cuticle work, a clean line along the nail fold and colour laid close to the skin — so the finish looks tidy on day one and graceful weeks later.

Every appointment is one person, one chair and full attention. Shape, length and shade are chosen with you, never for you.

Manicure & pedicure in Tirana

At RM Nails Suite, we believe that hand and foot care is more than a routine — it’s a calming ritual of beauty and self-care. Our professional manicure and pedicure services in Tirana are designed to bring comfort, precision, and elegance to every detail.

We offer classic, combined, and gel manicures, as well as pedicure and foot spa treatments for smooth, healthy skin. Each session includes careful nail shaping, cuticle care, gentle exfoliation, and nourishing massage to restore softness and glow.
